# DEMO SCRIPT — TCS Leasing CRM (work in progress)

Full 7-minute scenario lands in Phase 5. Recorded decisions for the demo:

- The legacy funnel «Продажи Knewit» stays in the database, but we never open
  it during the demo. The TCS Leasing pipeline is the single default, so the
  board and analytics always open on it. Do not click the pipeline selector.
- All data is fictional (source='demo', 555-01xx phones, "123 Demo St,
  Philadelphia, PA", surnames "Demo"). The banner «DEMO — fictional data» is
  always visible in the top bar.
- Estimations are labelled "Estimate" and come from editable assumptions.
